At The Oak House School, we believe that every child deserves a nurturing and challenging environment to grow and thrive. As co-principals, Jenifer Shahda and McCalla Hill are dedicated to providing a world-class education that prepares our students for success in all aspects of life. Our Programs

A comprehensive curriculum that challenges, inspires, and prepares every student.

🎒

Pre-Kindergarten

Our Pre-Kindergarten program is designed for children aged 4-5, offering a play-based curriculum that introduces early literacy, math, and social skills.

📚

Elementary

Our Elementary program serves students in grades K-5, with a focus on developing foundational skills in reading, writing, math, and science.

🔬

Middle School

Our Middle School program serves students in grades 6-8, with a focus on developing critical thinking, problem-solving, and communication skills.
